Sound familiar?

Monday morning, in almost every NGO office.

Reports, posts, minutes, member updates, translations. Small teams carry a mountain of writing, and staff are quietly turning to AI tools to cope, often with no rules and no training.

The gap

Almost everyone uses AI. Almost no one has rules for it.

The largest survey of humanitarian workers on AI asked 2,539 people in 144 countries. Individual use is nearly universal; organizational readiness is not.

That gap is where the risks live: invented statistics in donor reports, beneficiary data pasted into public tools, and time savings no one can prove. Closing it is what this programme does.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do our staff need any AI experience?

No. Session 1 starts from zero. Learners need a smartphone or computer with internet and a free account with the AI tool chosen for the cohort.

Which AI tools do you teach?

Skills are taught by task, not by brand, so they work with any capable assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. We pick one main tool and one backup with you, tested on the phones your staff actually use.

Is our data safe during the training?

Yes. No personal or confidential data is used in any exercise. Learners practise with non-confidential documents and learn the red, amber, green rule before they paste anything. The course covers Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act and your own policies.

In which language is it delivered?

Swahili or English, or both. Templates and handouts are provided in the delivery language.

In person or online?

Either. In person in Dar es Salaam, or live online for teams spread across regions. Sessions can run weekly or be compressed into two days.

Do learners get a certificate?

Yes, a certificate of completion for learners who attend at least three of four sessions, complete the hands-on tasks, submit a time log and pass the final assessment.
